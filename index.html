About

My work moves between theatre, video and sound — always led by voice.

Polish performer and vocalist between Berlin and Amsterdam. I began with Marta Górnicka's Choir of Women in Warsaw, touring that work through Tokyo, Zurich and New Delhi — a long way of saying I learned to work with my voice in a group before I learned to use it alone.

Since then: theatre and choir work with the Berliner Ensemble and HAU, performance and video with Gaweda & Kulbokaite, Colin Self, Lara Damaso and Monica Mirabiles, and my own work, including G0L3M with Thilo Garus. I studied Theatre Studies at Freie Universität Berlin and I'm now at SAE Institute Amsterdam, building my own record.
